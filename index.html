About Me

I am a highly motivated Biomedical Engineering student at Arizona State University with a deep personal passion for improving human health and mobility. Having witnessed loved ones struggle with cancer and physical limitations, I am driven to develop innovative solutions — from regenerative medicine and prosthetics to cybernetic augmentation — that enhance quality of life and extend healthy lifespans.

My background combines hands-on lab experience (molecular biology, 3D modeling, circuit design), programming (Python, Java, C++, MATLAB, Arduino), and real-world leadership (4-time Employee of the Year, Safety Team Leader). I thrive in collaborative environments and love turning complex ideas into working prototypes.

When I'm not studying or coding, you’ll find me designing RPG games in RPG Maker MZ, building electronic projects, or volunteering to give back to the community that shaped me.
